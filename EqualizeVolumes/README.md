# EqualizeVolumes

Sets the same volume for everyone in a voice channel, in one click.

## How to use

1. Right-click a member in a voice channel and choose **Equalize everyone's volume**.
2. Pick a level with the slider, a preset (25, 50, 75, 100 %) or by typing it, then click **Apply**.

The window opens on the last value you used. Keyboard: type a percentage, **Shift + ↑/↓** for ±10 %, **Enter** to apply, **Esc** to close.

## Auto mode

Turn it on in the same window to give this volume to people who join your voice channel, and to those already there when you join.

- People you adjusted by hand are left alone.
- To keep someone out of it, right-click them and choose **Exclude from auto equalize**. The list of excluded people is in the plugin settings.

Volumes are your local user volumes: only what you hear changes.
