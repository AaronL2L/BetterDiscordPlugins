# FriendsSortAssistant

Helps you clean up your friends list by suggesting people you no longer talk to.

## How to use

Open **Friends** and click **Sort friends** in the top bar. The window lists suggestions you can filter by:
- **last conversation** (more than 30 days, 3 months, 6 months, 1 year, or no direct messages only);
- **friendship age** (no minimum, 15 days, 1 month, 3 months);
- **servers in common** (only friends you share no server with).

Each card shows the reason, how long you have been friends and your servers in common, with two buttons:
- **Send a message** opens your DM with that person.
- **Remove friend** removes that one person after a 5-second countdown you can cancel. Closing the window cancels every pending removal.

Nothing is removed automatically: every removal is one click on one person.
