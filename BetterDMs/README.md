# BetterDMs

Shows the last message of each conversation in your DM list, like on mobile: who wrote it, when, and who is typing.

## What you see

Under each name in your DM list:
- the **last message**, with its emojis and formatting. It starts with **You:** when you wrote it, and with the author's name in a group;
- a short description for messages without text: **2 images**, **GIF**, **Voice message**, **File: name**, **Missed call**, **Bob left the group**…;
- the **time** on the right: **now**, **5 min**, the time of day, **Yesterday**, a weekday or a date;
- **Typing…** while someone is writing (**Bob is typing…** in a group).

Unread conversations are in bold. Messages from blocked users show **Blocked message**. If [MaskedWords](../MaskedWords) is on, the words you hide stay hidden in the previews.

## Options

In the plugin settings, under **DM list**:
- **Show the last message**
- **Show the time**
- **Show who is typing**
- **Activity first:** when a friend is playing or listening to something, show that instead of the last message.

Previews of older conversations are loaded the way Discord's own overlay loads them, 30 conversations at a time. The plugin never sends anything for you.
